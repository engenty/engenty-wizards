engenty wizards is in a closed beta: a new account needs an invitation code. "Get started" on
[engenty.ai](https://engenty.ai) takes your e-mail and puts you on the list; the code comes by
mail as soon as a seat is free. With a code, the same step opens the sign-up.

## Sign up

1. Type the code at [account.engenty.ai/sign-up](https://account.engenty.ai/sign-up). The link
   in the invitation mail has it filled in.
2. Sign in with Google, GitHub, or a link by mail. There is no password.
3. The studio opens at [engenty.ai/studio](https://engenty.ai/studio).

Your account is a **team**: it holds your credits, your spaces and, on the Team plan, your
colleagues. It starts with credits, some of which end on a date; the account pages at
[account.engenty.ai](https://account.engenty.ai) show them.

## The first visit

The studio asks four things, one per page. Each but the first can be skipped and changed later.

| Page | Asks | Goes to |
|---|---|---|
| Who you are | What your team is called: your company, your club, or just your name | The team's name at account.engenty.ai |
| About you & your brand | Your website. engenty reads your name, what you do, your logo and colours from it; or you write a line about yourself | [Space → Info & brand](./space.md#info--brand) |
| Knowledge | Documents the wizards may look things up in: price lists, your terms, an FAQ | [Space → Knowledge](./space.md#knowledge) |
| Your space | Nothing: it explains the space that holds all of this, "Meine Wizards" | — |

Then "Build your first wizard" opens the studio's start: describe a task in a sentence, or pick
a template. See [Build a wizard](./build-a-wizard.md).

## What the cloud does

- Wizards built here run behind a link that works day and night, on the team's credits.
- Share links, the tag for your website, QR codes and the channels of [Test and share](./test-and-share.md) work as on an install.
- What the wizards think with is the team's: [Models and account](./models.md).

## Also on your computer

The same account works on your own computer: [install](./install.md) engenty wizards there and
sign in during the setup. It thinks with your Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or Cursor subscription,
reads your local files and browses for you; what you publish there runs in the cloud too, behind
the same links. "Also on your computer" on the studio's start page mails you the command.
