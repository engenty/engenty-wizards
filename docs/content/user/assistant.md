The assistant stands at the bottom of every page of the studio but the editor, which has a
conversation of its own. It thinks on the High class of Text & reasoning: your subscription,
your keys or credits ([Models and account](./models.md)).

| Ask it | It |
|---|---|
| How something works | Reads this guide (the version you installed) and links the page |
| Where something is | Opens the page and points at the place |
| About your account | Looks up your plan, credits and what you used; it never buys or upgrades |
| About templates | Searches the gallery |
| About your wizards | Looks at their runs, the failed ones too |
| About models | Says what thinks and makes here, and what is missing |
| To fill in the space | Reads a website or your files ([Space](./space.md#have-it-filled-in)) |

It sees the page you write from: where you are, what is open and what the page shows. It may put
a value into a field for you; you check it and save. It does not press buttons.

## Where it stands

| | |
|---|---|
| At the bottom | The default. Click the field or the engenty, and the chat rises above it |
| Small | The engenty at the lower right; a click opens the chat beside it. Data and Results keep it small |
| Sidebar | Beside the page, on a window 1,520 pixels wide or more |

The buttons in the chat's header switch between them; the studio remembers your choice.

## One chat

You have one chat, the same on every page and in every space, after a reload and on another
device. Nobody else in the team sees it.

- **New chat** (the menu at the chat's name) closes the chat with a short summary and starts an
  empty one. The assistant keeps the summaries of your last chats in mind.
- **Earlier chats** lists the closed ones to read. Ask about something you discussed before, and
  the assistant searches them.

## Live chat

**Talk** stands where Send does while the field is empty. The chat becomes a call: you speak, a
pause sends what you said, and the answer is read aloud. Then it listens again; talking over it
stops its voice.

- Chrome and Safari turn your speech into text themselves. Other browsers record, and your
  model for voice notes writes it down.
- The answer is read with your computer's voices, or with the speech model where one is set up
  (the speaker button in the call).
- Everything said stays in the chat as messages.
